# The 2025 Imsil earthquake swarm, southwestern Korea

Analysis of the small earthquake swarm that occurred at mid-crustal depth beneath Imsil, southwestern Korea, on 2 November 2025 (UTC). The Korea Meteorological Administration (KMA) listed 24 events, ML 1.0–1.9, within 4.5 hours.

**Summary report:** [`DL_detection/docs/reports/imsil_2025_summary_20261014/report.pdf`](DL_detection/docs/reports/imsil_2025_summary_20261014/report.pdf), 11 pages with 7 figures. Every number in it is produced by notebook 16 (`DL_detection/notebooks/16_report_figures.ipynb`).

## What was done

The catalog was rebuilt with the detection and relocation pipeline adopted for the 2020–2026 Haenam sequences, changing only the region values:

1. **Detection and location.** Deep-learning phase picking (PhaseNet family), PyOcto association and HYPOINVERSE, from 1 September 2025 to 31 January 2026 at 54 KMA stations within 100 km.
2. **Relocation.** Double-difference relocation with waveform cross-correlation and 1,000 bootstrap replicas. The reference catalog keeps the events whose 95 % location half-widths are at most 25 m.
3. **Local magnitudes.** The Sheen et al. (2018) scale.
4. **Template matching.** A network matched filter (FMF) with the 28 reference events, followed by a joint relocation of the detections.
5. **Comparisons and follow-ups.**
   - Migration and diffusion fronts.
   - A comparison with Park et al. (2026, *Physics of the Earth and Planetary Interiors* 381, 107613).
   - A template-matching scan of 2018–2026.
   - Relocation of three earlier events (15 April 2019) found by that scan.

Each choice not settled by the Haenam rules has its evidence in a numbered notebook and its status in `DL_detection/docs/decisions.csv`.

## Main results (details and uncertainties in the report)

- **Catalog.** Template matching finds 235 events, 207 of them new. All but one occur within about 5 hours on 2–3 November 2025.
- **Geometry.** The 101 best-located events lie on a near-vertical east–west plane (strike 268.7°, dip 88.2°, RMS thickness 7.4 m).
- **Migration.** The swarm spread east and up the plane, consistent with a diffusion front of D = 0.50–0.63 m²/s (fit windows of 0.5–2 h).
- **Earlier activity.** Three small events on 15 April 2019, not in the KMA catalog, match the swarm templates. Relocated with the swarm, they lie 266–305 m south of the 2025 plane, at the same depths: a parallel strand active six years earlier.

## Not included

- Waveform data. KMA waveforms are not redistributed.
- Instrument-response files and the KMA catalog (third-party).
- Third-party code and model weights (listed with sources and checksums in `_external/PROVENANCE.tsv`).
- Run directories, which are regenerated by the scripts.
- Server addresses in the transfer scripts are replaced by placeholders such as `<mara-host>`.

Paths in the scripts are absolute paths on the analysis machine.
